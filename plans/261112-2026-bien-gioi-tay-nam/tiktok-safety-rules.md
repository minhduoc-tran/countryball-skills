# TikTok safety rules (binding for script, images, every shot, captions)

Goal: an educational history video that TikTok's Community Guidelines (violent & graphic content, hate speech,
violent extremism, misinformation) and its keyword/visual filters will not flag, restrict or demote.

## Images (photos + animation)
- NO corpses, blood, wounds, skulls, bones, bone piles, ossuary interiors (Ba Chúc / Choeung Ek skull towers), torture
  tools, executions, Tuol Sleng (S-21) prisoner mugshots or victim portraits, starving/emaciated people, children in
  distress, mass-grave pits. Memorials only as exterior/architecture (stupa silhouette, incense, flowers, candles).
- NO realistic weapon aimed at a person, no firing at people, no muzzle-flash close-ups. War = map arrows, tank/truck
  icons moving, distant smoke columns, artillery as far flashes on a map. Countryballs: no hitting, no blood; losses =
  bandage, sad face, dim light, candle.
- NO Khmer Rouge symbols glorified: their DK flag only small on a map/ball, never as a hero image; no KR propaganda photo.
  Pol Pot: name card + plain silhouette or a neutral archive photo only (no caricature, no "evil" glorification).
- Flags correct and respectful; no flag burning/stepping on.
- Photos: free licence (PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA) with credit on screen. Prefer: maps, landscapes, buildings, memorials
  today, ceremonies, people celebrating/returning home, soldiers marching/withdrawal parades, documents.

## Words (narration, on-screen text, captions, hashtags)
- Avoid graphic/violent verbs and gore words: "giết", "chém", "tàn sát", "thảm sát", "xử bắn", "đẫm máu", "máu",
  "xác", "đầu lâu", "hành quyết", "tra tấn", "băm", "đập đầu". Also avoid "diệt", "tiêu diệt", "đánh tan xác",
  "quét sạch", "nghiền nát".
- Use instead: "thiệt mạng", "mất", "ra đi", "nạn nhân", "tội ác", "thảm kịch", "đau thương", "bị sát hại" (max 1-2
  times, factual), "chế độ Pôn Pốt" / "chính quyền Khơ Me Đỏ" / "Khơ Me Đỏ"; military: "phản công", "đẩy lùi",
  "tiến vào", "giải phóng", "giành lại", "bảo vệ".
- "Diệt chủng": allowed at most once, in a sober factual sentence (it is the historical term: "chế độ diệt chủng"),
  NEVER in on-screen text, cover, caption or hashtags.
- No hate: always target the REGIME ("Khơ Me Đỏ", "chế độ Pôn Pốt"), never the Khmer/Cambodian people. Cambodian
  people are shown as victims, friends, the ones who rebuild. No slurs, no "bọn", "lũ", "quân giặc ... Miên".
  Avoid "Miên" entirely. Today: friendship VN-Campuchia.
- No glorifying war or calls to violence; tone = remembrance + peace. Ending stresses peace, friendship and the people
  who fell (VN soldiers + Cambodian victims).
- Third countries (China, USA, Thailand, ASEAN, UN seat): neutral, factual, minimal; no insults, no current-politics
  takes. Disputed numbers ALWAYS with source on screen ("theo phía Việt Nam", "ước tính", "các nguồn khác nhau").
- No misinformation: every date/number in `science` with a source.
- Caption/hashtags: neutral (#LichSu #LichSuVietNam #Campuchia #Countryball #HoaBinh), no #war-gore style tags.
