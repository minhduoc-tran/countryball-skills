## 1. Context: Khơ Me Đỏ cầm quyền (1975-1979)

- [C] 17/4/1975 KR enter Phnôm Pênh; cities emptied "in less than a week"; money, markets, private property abolished; schools, hospitals, monasteries closed. — https://www.britannica.com/place/Cambodia/Civil-war ; https://www.britannica.com/event/Cambodian-genocide ; https://www.qdnd.vn/40nam-chien-thang-chien-tranh-bao-ve-bien-gioi-tay-nam/phan-tich-binh-luan/bai-1-lo-dien-bo-mat-phan-dong-cua-tap-doan-pon-pot-559438 ("xóa bỏ thành thị... xóa bỏ quan hệ tiền tệ, buôn bán")
- [C] 5/1/1976 Constitution proclaims "Democratic Kampuchea" (Campuchia Dân chủ). — https://countrystudies.us/cambodia/31.htm ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Kampuchea (Wikidata: promulgated 14/12/1975, effective 5/1/1976)
- [C] Duration "3 năm 8 tháng 20 ngày" (17/4/1975 - 7/1/1979) - standard VN + Cambodian phrase. — qdnd Bài 1 (above) ; https://baoquocte.vn/su-kien-trong-dai-trong-quan-he-viet-nam-campuchia-14779.html ; AKP via https://www.vietnamplus.vn/ky-niem-47-nam-chien-thang-71-hoi-sinh-campuchia-sau-tham-hoa-diet-chung-post1087124.vnp
- Death toll 1975-79 (sources differ; use range):
  - [C] ~1,7 million (~21% of pop.) — Yale CGP (Kiernan): https://macmillan.yale.edu/gsp/introduction-cambodian-genocide-program ; Britannica "at least 1.5 million, ~20%": https://www.britannica.com/event/Cambodian-genocide
  - [U] 2-2,5 million, "most likely 2,2 m" (Etcheson, DC-Cam); demographic range 1,17-3,42 m (Heuveline) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodian_genocide
  - [C] DC-Cam mapping: ~23.745 mass-grave pits, ~1,3 m suspected execution victims (rest: hunger, overwork, disease) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_Fields ; https://macmillan.yale.edu/gsp/report-cambodian-genocide-program-1997-1999
  - [U] VN press: "gần 3 triệu" (qdnd Bài 1) / "hơn 2 triệu" (baoquocte). → Do NOT use 3 million on screen. Use "khoảng 1,7 - 2 triệu (ước tính)".
- [U] Phnôm Pênh population evacuated ~2 million (commonly cited; not verified here) → say "hàng triệu người dân thành phố".

## 3. 1977 escalation

- [C] Night 30/4/1977 (VN celebrating 2 years of reunification) KR attack whole An Giang border: 14/16 border communes; by 19/5/1977 222 killed, 614 wounded (VN figures). Defended by Vĩnh Xương, Vĩnh Gia, Nhân Hưng militia + Div 330 regiment. — qdnd Bài 2 ; qdnd Bài 1 ; https://baolaocai.vn/bai-1-su-phan-boi-cua-tap-doan-pon-pot-va-che-do-khmer-do-post277398.html
- [C] Night 24 → dawn 25/9/1977 (commune was preparing children's Trung thu festivities; [U] - lunar 15/8 fell ~27/9/1977) KR attack Tân Biên & Bến Cầu (Tây Ninh); xã Tân Lập worst: 506 killed, 135 wounded, 20 families wiped out (VN); Báo Tây Ninh later: 592. qdnd: ">1.000 dân" in 3 communes. 11 teachers of Tân Lập primary school among victims. — https://nhandan.vn/tay-ninh-nhung-ngay-chien-dau-bao-ve-bien-gioi-tay-nam-post345919.html ; https://vov.vn/chinh-tri/40-nam-chien-thang-bao-ve-bien-gioi-tay-nam-ky-uc-tay-ninh-860498.vov ; qdnd Bài 2. On screen: "hơn 500 người dân xã Tân Lập".
- [C] From 25/9/1977 KR mass 9 divisions along An Giang, Kiên Giang, Long An, Đồng Tháp, Tây Ninh (VN). — qdnd Bài 2
- [C] 23/5/1977 VN Central Military Commission order: defend territory "đồng thời tôn trọng chủ quyền lãnh thổ của Campuchia". — qdnd Bài 2
- [C] 5/12/1977 - 5/1/1978 VN counter-attack: 8 divisions (Corps 3, 4, MR 7, 9) on Routes 7, 1, 2, pursue 20-30 km into Cambodia, then withdraw (nights 4-5/1/1978). — qdnd Bài 2 ; https://baoquankhu7.vn (Bài 2 "Giữ từng tấc đất", via search) ; Western: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodian%E2%80%93Vietnamese_War
- [C] 31/12/1977 DK (Khieu Samphan / Phnom Penh Radio) "temporarily" severs diplomatic relations with VN, accuses VN of aggression; VN Gov't issues own statement same day. — qdnd Bài 2 ; https://m.tapchiqptd.vn/vi/events-and-comments/victory-of-7-january-1979-a-noble-symbol-of-vietnam-cambodia-fighting-solidarity-21330.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Kampuchea

## 4. Ba Chúc (An Giang), 18-30/4/1978 - facts only, NO remains on screen

- [C] 18/4/1978 KR cross border, encircle xã Ba Chúc (huyện Tri Tôn, An Giang, ~6,4 km from border); 3.157 civilians lost their lives, mostly women, elderly, children (VN; also used by Wikipedia/int'l). — https://nld.com.vn/chinh-tri/tuong-niem-3157-nan-nhan-bi-tham-sat-tai-ba-chuc-20230505152658727.htm ; https://btlsqsvn.mod.gov.vn/di-tich-quan-su/chi-tiet/:slugId?relic_id=4a2fc507-4c55-4e02-bb0e-a4ca91913f6e ; https://tand.angiang.gov.vn/?action=tinchitiet&idbaiviet=193&idloai=1 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ba_Ch%C3%BAc_massacre
- [U] End date: most sources 30/4/1978 (12 days); Military History Museum/NLĐ: 29/4 (11-12 ngày đêm). On screen: "tháng 4/1978" or "18 - 30/4/1978".
- [C] People sheltered in pagodas Tam Bửu & Phi Lai; site = "Nhà mồ Ba Chúc - chùa Tam Bửu - chùa Phi Lai". — angiang tand (above) ; https://baoangiang.com.vn/nha-mo-ba-chuc-ban-cao-trang-bi-thuong-va-thong-diep-ve-khat-vong-hoa-binh-a484446.html

## Story beats (13, in order)

1. COLD OPEN - trưa 7/1/1979, Phnôm Pênh: a T-54 icon rolls over Monivong bridge into a silent, empty city; a Cambodian woman steps out of a doorway. "Thành phố gần như không còn ai. Vì sao?"
2. REWIND 17/4/1975: KR ball enters; cities emptied, money/markets/schools gone; "3 năm 8 tháng 20 ngày", ~1,7-2 triệu người ra đi (ước tính).
3. Same month, the sea: 3/5 Phú Quốc, 10/5 Thổ Chu, >500 residents taken. VN retakes island 27/5 - then chooses talks (1976) - hook: "Việt Nam đã nhịn".
4. 30/4/1977: An Giang border lit up on the 2nd reunification anniversary; Tân Lập 24-25/9/1977, days before Trung thu (a child's lantern going dark).
5. Hun Sen crosses the border 20/6/1977 - the regime's own commanders begin to flee.
6. Dec 1977: VN pushes back 20-30 km, then withdraws; 31/12/1977 KR cut relations.
7. 5/2/1978: VN's peace offer (ceasefire, 5 km, int'l supervision) - rejected (paper torn by wind, not by hand).
8. Ba Chúc 4/1978: 3.157 - memorial exterior, white lotus, incense. Quiet beat.
9. Logic beat "Vì sao phải hành động": KR 19 divisions on border, 10 attack 23/12; Front founded 2/12/1978 at Snuol calls for help.
10. 15 days: map arrows on Routes 1, 7, 2; Neak Leung ferry one tank at a time; Tà Lơn landing → Kampong Som 10/1; Phnôm Pênh 7/1; whole country 17/1.
11. Rebirth: 8/1 new council; 18/2 treaty; markets, schools, pagodas reopen; riel 20/3/1980; "ngày sinh lần thứ hai".
12. The long 10 years: famine relief, guerrilla war on Thai border, isolation (1 neutral line); withdrawals from 1982.
13. Going home: 25/9/1989 farewell at Independence Monument (>100.000 people), 26/9 last column crosses border; "hàng chục nghìn" did not return → remains still being brought home (850 in 2025-26). End: Friendship Monument / Paris 1991 / peace today, candle for both peoples.
