# Images report: Biên giới Tây Nam / Việt Nam - Khơ Me Đỏ 1975-1989

Folder: `public/bien-gioi-tay-nam/img/`. It holds 59 JPEG photos and maps, plus 4 flag PNGs, 63 files in total (29.9 MB, 28.5 MiB). Every file is listed in `manifest.json`.
- Long side is at most 1920 px. JPEGs are q85 and stripped. Nothing was upscaled. Files came from Commons thumbnails (same file, same licence). Originals and scratch files were deleted.
- Each licence was checked through the Commons API (extmetadata LicenseShortName + Artist). Credits are the Artist field with HTML stripped.
- Licences: CC BY-SA 4.0 (20), PD (18), CC BY-SA 3.0 (9), CC0 (5), CC BY 2.0 (3), CC BY-SA 2.0 (2), CC BY 4.0 (2), CC BY 3.0 (1), CC BY-SA 3.0 nl (1), "Attribution" (2).
  - The 2 "Attribution" files are free with credit: the Fototeca photo of Pol Pot and the Copernicus Sentinel-2 image of Thổ Chu.
- Excluded on purpose: AP, Getty, VNA and UN photos. Suspected copyvios were also excluded, for example a 300 px "Vietnamese soldiers entering Phnom Penh 1979" uploaded as "own work", and an AP photo tagged CC BY.
- Edits: `toa-nha-tuol-sleng-ben-ngoai.jpg` was cropped to remove the wooden frame in the foreground. The sign beside Nhà mồ Ba Chúc says "bọn Pôn Pốt thảm sát", so the 9:16 crop must leave it out (noted in the manifest).
- I opened all 63 files on contact sheets. The risky ones I opened at full size: Choeung Ek, Tuol Sleng, Ba Chúc ×2, Phi Lai, the Kampot and Kratie statues, Pol Pot, the Khao-I-Dang camp, the 1979 aid ship and the Monivong recon photo. None shows remains, wounds or distress.
