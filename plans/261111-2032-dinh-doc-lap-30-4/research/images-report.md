## Not used, and why
- Hubert van Es / UPI rooftop helicopter (File:Helicopter evacuation of Saigon atop 22 Gia Long Street.webp): Commons tags it PD, but it is a UPI photo. Excluded by rule. Replaced with the rooftop today.
- Tank crashing the gate and the flag on the roof, 30/4 (TTXVN / Françoise Demulder / Sygma / AP): no free licence. Recreate with countryball animation.
- Photos of 30/4 crowds and liberation-army troops (TTXVN, Vietnamese photographers, 1975): still under copyright (75 years from publication). None on Commons has a clear free licence.
- Dirck Halstead photos ("CH-53 landing at DAO", "refugees on US ship"): tagged PD, but Halstead was a TIME/Gamma freelancer. Status unclear, skipped.
- "Surrender letter of Duong Van Minh.JPG": the uploader's own PD claim for a museum document photo. Unclear, skipped.
- RVN Presidential Press Office photos of Thiệu (1971): Commons says PD, but Vietnamese law gives photos 75 years. Unsafe, so a US government photo was used instead.
- Museum painting of the 1975 victory (photo by RG72) and the 2025 "30-4" street poster (Nat4Eben): the photos are CC, but the artwork is copyrighted. Skipped.
- "Cong Vao Dinh Thong Nhat" gate photo (450x600): too small for video.
- 2015 New Year fireworks (panoramio): not 30/4. Skipped to avoid misleading footage.
